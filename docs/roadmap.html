Where it stands today

The prototype proves the format

Version 0.3 is one HTML page with its data written into the code. It has 72 terms, 13 datasheets (shoulder girdle and plexus brachialis), the 12 cranial nerves, the KI course path, 28 quiz questions with explanations, a 1-3-7-14-30 day review schedule and an AI tutor. Learning content can now be shown in Hungarian and Swedish side by side.

That covers roughly 3–5% of what a complete platform needs. The main limits are structural: content sits inside the code, the interface text is still mostly Hungarian, progress lives in one browser, and no expert has reviewed anything. Phase 0 fixes the structure before content volume grows twentyfold.

Content pipeline

How every item is made

The volume only works with a repeatable pipeline. Claude drafts and translates; people verify. Nothing is marked as reviewed until a human has checked it.

1 · DRAFTHungarian draftFrom open sources (OpenStax A&P, CC BY 4.0) and the KI course plans, following the datasheet templates.
2 · TERMSLatin checkEvery Latin name checked against Terminologia Anatomica (TA2).
3 · SWEDISHSwedish versionTranslated, then terms checked against Svensk MeSH (KI University Library).
4 · QUESTIONSQuestion setAt least 10 questions per datasheet, each wrong answer explained, linked to its source item.
5 · REVIEWExpert reviewA reviewer checks the content and sets the status to reviewed, with their name and the date.
6 · RELEASEPublishUnreviewed items stay visible but carry a "draft" marker in the app.
Data model

One record per structure, three languages per field

Every text field holds all three languages, so the Hungarian and Swedish versions can never drift apart. A build check fails if any field is missing its Swedish text. Relations (innervated by, acts on joint, tested by) are links between records, not free text, so the quiz can generate "which nerve…" questions automatically.

Record types: term, muscle, joint, bone, nerve, spinal segment, organ system, physiology topic, condition, outcome measure, lesson, question, clinical case, media asset.

Decisions for you

Four choices that shape Phase 0

Platform

A web app that installs on the phone and works offline (recommended, one codebase), or native iOS and Android apps (more work, App Store review).

Who reviews

At least one Swedish-speaking physiotherapist or anatomy teacher and one Hungarian conductor or physiotherapist. Without them, everything stays marked as draft.

Who uses it

Only you, or classmates too. Sharing it means accounts and progress sync, and it makes the image-licence rules stricter.

Scope of conductive education

Whether Pető-method content stays in this app as its own track, or moves to a separate product later.

Next two weeks

Concrete first steps

  1. Decide the four questions above. They determine the Phase 0 architecture.
  2. Move content out of the code into JSON files following the data model, starting with today's 13 datasheets, 12 cranial nerves and 28 questions.
  3. Translate the remaining interface text into Swedish (buttons, status messages, statistics), so the Swedish-only mode is complete.
